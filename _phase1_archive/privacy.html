Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

At Veruthe Alla Kudumba Unit, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our website or participate in our events.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information: Name, email address, phone number (when you contact us, register for events, or join as a member)
  • Event data: Attendance history, preferences, dietary requirements
  • Payment information: Processed securely through third-party payment providers (we do not store card details)
  • Website usage: Basic analytics data like pages visited and device type
  • Photos and videos: Taken during events for promotional purposes

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Communicate with you about events, membership, and community updates
  • Process event registrations and payments
  • Improve our events and services
  • Share event photos and videos on our social media and website
  • Send you updates about upcoming events (you can opt out anytime)

4. Data Sharing

We don't sell your data. Period.

We may share limited information with:

  • Event venues: Headcount and dietary requirements for planning purposes
  • Payment processors: To securely handle transactions
  • Legal obligations: If required by law

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We do our best, but we can't guarantee absolute security.

6. Photos & Media

By attending our events, you consent to being photographed or filmed. These images may be used on our website, social media, and marketing materials.

If you'd prefer not to be in photos, let us know before the event and we'll do our best to accommodate your request. If you find a photo of yourself that you'd like removed, just reach out and we'll take it down.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Request removal of your photos from our platforms

8. Cookies

Our website may use basic cookies for functionality and analytics. These help us understand how people use our site so we can make it better. No creepy tracking — we promise.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites (Instagram, WhatsApp, Google Maps, etc.). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party sites.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
