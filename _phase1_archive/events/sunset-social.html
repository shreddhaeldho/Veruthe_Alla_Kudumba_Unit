What's happening?

An evening of sunsets, new connections, and conversations that don't feel forced. We'll set the scene — you just need to show up.

No awkward icebreakers that make you want to leave. Just a relaxed space where people actually talk to each other, play some games, share food, and watch the sun go down together.

Whether you come alone or with friends, you'll leave with stories and probably a few new group chats.
